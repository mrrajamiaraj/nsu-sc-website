## How the public site is put together

| Concern | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Root shell | `app/layout.tsx` | Fonts: Geist and Geist Mono (local files), Barlow Condensed (Google). Site metadata, with the base URL from `lib/constants.ts`. |
| Public layout | `app/(site)/layout.tsx` | `.site-type`, `ScrollSportsBackground`, `Navbar`, `PageTransition`, `Footer`. Reads the `site_logo` and `contact_email` settings. The `(site)` group keeps the admin out of the public chrome. |
| Global styles | `app/globals.css`, `tailwind.config.ts` | Fixed teal wash, condensed uppercase h1/h2 inside `.site-type`, `.glass-panel`, `.nav-shell`, `.photo-backdrop`, `.text-gradient-brand`, `.tap-target`. The brand gradient runs `#3fcfca → #04a49f → #004b49`. |
| Navigation | `components/layout/Navbar.tsx`, `Footer.tsx` | Icon links at `md` and up; a toggle menu below that. The footer's social links are hardcoded (A11). |
| Content source | `lib/data/*.ts` → `lib/supabase/server.ts` | Cookie-bound Supabase client, so every public page renders dynamically on each request (A17). |
| Editable copy | `site_content` via `lib/data/site-content.ts` | Home, About, Teams stats, Contact, logo, featured event. Pages also have hardcoded fallback text. |
| Hardcoded facts | `lib/constants.ts` | `FOUNDING_YEAR = 1995` and the default contact email `sports@northsouth.edu` |
| Image upload | `lib/storage.ts` | sharp resizes to fit 1920px and saves WebP at quality 80, stored in Cloudflare R2 (`nsusc-media`). Video is uploaded as-is. |
| Image delivery | `next.config.mjs` | `images.unoptimized: true`, so images are served at their stored size. Remote patterns allow Supabase storage and R2. |
| Static images | `public/images/**` (1.4 MB) | Crest, plus `about/our-story.png`, `teams/*.png` and `events/*.png`. These appear to be stock photos (A7). |
| Admin publishing | `app/admin/dashboard/**/actions.ts` | Server actions call `revalidatePath()` on the matching public routes. Registration revalidates only `/`. Because public pages are dynamic, revalidation currently has no visible effect. |
| Middleware | `middleware.ts`, `lib/supabase/middleware.ts` | Matcher is `/admin/:path*` only. Public reads still refresh any Supabase session cookie the browser sends. |
| Specs | `SRS.md`, `TECH_STACK.md`, `design reference/` | NFR-UI-1 (375/768/1920), NFR-16 (WCAG 2.1 AA), NFR-19 (friendly 404/500), NFR-20 (nothing hardcoded). TECH_STACK recommends Cloudflare Pages, but the live site is on Vercel. |

## Issues

Severity is the impact on a student, alumnus or sponsor trying to finish their task. **Verified** means seen in the browser or in the data. **Code** means read in source only. **Assumption** marks something the club needs to confirm.

### A1 · High · Verified: Blog posts are invented placeholders
- **Routes:** `/blog`, `/blog/[postId]`, and the home blog strip. All widths.
- **Observed:** All 4 posts have an empty `content` field. They're about teams that don't exist ("Thunder Lions", "Storm Strikers", "Hoop Warriors") and use stock covers from `public/images/teams/*.png`. They come from `supabase/migrations/0005_seed.sql`.
- **Steps:** Open `/blog/dcc4893b-7026-492f-8189-31cca8a84963` at 390. Below the cover image, the next section is "More from the blog".
- **Screenshot:** [s11](2026-11-27-baseline/s11-sparse-details-390.jpg)

### A2 · High · Verified: Every Register button opens a placeholder form URL
- **Routes:** Home hero, home join band, and the closing blocks on Events, Teams, Achievements, About and Blog.
- **Observed:** `registration_settings.google_form_url = https://forms.google.com/nsusc-membership` (the seed value). Real links look like `docs.google.com/forms/…` or `forms.gle/…`. **Assumption:** it doesn't reach a club form; I didn't open it.
- **Fix type:** Data, in Admin → Registration. In code, also check that the URL is a Google Forms link.

### A3 · High · Verified: Fixtures & results has no fixtures and doesn't say so
- **Route:** `/events`. All widths.
- **Observed:** All 26 events are Finished. The intro says "Register for what's coming up", but `RegistrationCarousel` returns `null` when nothing is upcoming (`components/events/RegistrationCarousel.tsx:121`), so no empty-state message appears.
- **Screenshot:** [s08](2026-11-27-baseline/s08-baseline-1440-1024-768-390.jpg)

### A4 · Medium · Verified: Sparse finished events are long pages of placeholders
- **Routes:** `/events/e2707aed…` (NPL 2024) and `/events/9f31c277…` (Carnival S01).
- **Observed:** 2,848px at 390. Season statistics say "not recorded", the prize pool shows "TBA", all three places say "Not announced", and the Winners and Photo gallery sections still render, with no result. The breadcrumbs read `Events › N › Season 1 (2024)` and `Events › NSCS › Season`.
- **Screenshots:** [s07](2026-11-27-baseline/s07-event-prize-tba-390.png), [s11](2026-11-27-baseline/s11-sparse-details-390.jpg)

### A5 · Medium · Verified: Home and About give different founding years
- **Observed:** Home shows "31 years as a club" from `FOUNDING_YEAR = 1995` (`lib/constants.ts` via `lib/data/stats.ts`). About shows "Established 1992" from `site_content.about_stat_established`. Also conflicts with NFR-20. **Assumption:** which year is right.

### A6 · Medium · Assumption: Club claims and stats with no visible source
- Teams: "8+ championships", "45+ medals", "72% win rate". About: "500+ members", "8+ championships", "Premier sports club in Bangladesh", "State-of-the-art training facilities". These are `site_content` values from migrations `0010` and `0012`. The club needs to confirm each one or remove it.

### A7 · Medium · Verified: Stock photos presented as club photos
- `public/images/about/our-story.png` (a sprinter in Brooks and Nike gear), `public/images/teams/football.png` (a women's league match with "Hero" boards), and the other `teams/*.png` and `events/*.png` files. They're used in About "Our story" and every blog cover. Real squad photos already exist in R2.

### A8 · Medium · Verified: Mobile menu is cut off in landscape and can't be dismissed with Escape
- **Observed:** At 667×375 the panel ends at y=566, so only 6 of 10 links are reachable: Members, Blog, Contact and Admin login are cut off. The header is fixed and the panel has no `max-height` or scrolling. Escape doesn't close it, and the page scrolls behind it. Source: `components/layout/Navbar.tsx`, lines 77–135.
- **Steps:** Use a 667×375 viewport on `/teams`, open the menu and try to reach Contact.
- **Screenshots:** [s02](2026-11-27-baseline/s02-menu-landscape-667x375.jpg), portrait for comparison [s01](2026-11-27-baseline/s01-menu-open-390-320.jpg)

### A9 · Medium · Verified: Gradient headings fade below readable contrast
- The `.text-gradient-brand` end color `#004b49` measures 1.51:1 on `#0c2b2a` and 1.17:1 on `#123f3d`. The start (7.88:1) and middle (4.89:1) pass. The last letters of short words fade out. WCAG AA needs 3:1 for large text (NFR-16).
- **Screenshot:** [s09](2026-11-27-baseline/s09-gradient-tail-1024.png)

### A10 · Medium · Verified: The 404 page is white and nearly unreadable
- With no `not-found.tsx`, Next's default 404 renders as faint text on white under the dark navbar, with no way back besides the navbar. Conflicts with NFR-19.
- **Screenshot:** [s10](2026-11-27-baseline/s10-404-and-320-smoke.jpg)

### A11 · Medium · Code: Footer social links go to generic home pages
- `components/layout/Footer.tsx:6-8` points to `https://facebook.com`, `https://instagram.com` and `https://youtube.com`.

### A12 · Low · Verified: Sponsors and alumni have no contact route
- The form only asks which squad, with no topic for sponsorship, alumni updates or trials. The details shown are the university's general contacts (`sports@northsouth.edu`, `+880 2-55668200`). **Assumption:** whether they reach the club panel.
- **Screenshot:** [s06](2026-11-27-baseline/s06-contact-handoff-390.jpg)

### A13 · Low · Verified: Profile dialog loses keyboard position
- On `/members` and `/alumni`, closing the dialog leaves focus on `BODY`, and Tab can leave the open dialog (`components/ui/ProfileModal.tsx`).
- **Screenshot:** [s04](2026-11-27-baseline/s04-member-dialog-390.jpg)

### A14 · Low · Verified: Alumni opens with all 143 profiles
- The default "All" view is 78,101px tall at 390 and 32,501px at 1440. Lazy loading keeps the first load light (about 5 image requests), and year selection works.
- **Screenshot:** [s05](2026-11-27-baseline/s05-alumni-year-390.jpg)

### A15 · Low · Verified: Images and the hero video ignore screen size and data cost
- Team photos up to 286 KB (1920px WebP) fill cards about 360px wide. Sampled /alumni portraits average about 36 KB. The home hero autoplays a 2.7 MB MP4 on phones, whatever the reduced-motion or data-saver setting.

### A16 · Low · Verified: Event filter labels truncate at 320
- The selects read "All seaso", "Any statu" and "Newest f". See [s10](2026-11-27-baseline/s10-404-and-320-smoke.jpg).

### A17 · Low · Verified: Public pages are uncached and can refresh admin sessions
- A cookie-bound client in `lib/supabase/server.ts` makes every route dynamic and queries Supabase on every view. A browser with a stale admin cookie makes an auth refresh call on every public page view; during this audit that led to a 429 rate limit from Supabase Auth. Changing this touches publishing, so it needs an explicitly approved phase.

## Unverified assumptions

- The placeholder form URL doesn't reach a real form (A2).
- Founding year: 1992 (About) or 1995 (code) (A5).
- The stats, superlatives (A6) and stock-image origins (A7).
- `sports@northsouth.edu` and `+880 2-55668200` reach the club panel (A12).
- The home "90 panel members" counts every `members` row, which may include archived panels.
- The registration-closed state renders as the code reads; it wasn't seen in a browser.
- Real devices (iOS Safari toolbar, Android back, slow 3G) weren't tested.

## Test notes

- Only public reads were made: page renders and anon-key REST `select`s. No form was submitted, no admin action was taken, and no data was written.
- A first sweep on `localhost:3000` carried a stale admin session cookie. That caused about 586 "Invalid Refresh Token" errors and a temporary 429 from Supabase Auth. Testing moved to `127.0.0.1`, which has no cookie.
- The dev server returned 404s for its own assets after a `/_not-found` recompile. It was restarted with a fresh `.next`, and measurements from that window were thrown away.
